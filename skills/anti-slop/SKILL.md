# Anti-slop Review
Flag repetitive card grids, unnecessary gradients, overused glassmorphism, decorative labels, meaningless icons, generic marketing copy, excessive buttons and identical section spacing. Replace with choices grounded in the site's actual content, brand and audience. Prefer restrained, specific composition over novelty for its own sake. Before edits, capture approved design decisions and compare before/after screenshots.
