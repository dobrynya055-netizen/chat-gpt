# Asset Quality
Use properly licensed images and fonts. Avoid upscaling low-resolution photos and stretching images beyond their native proportions. Select object-fit and object-position intentionally for each viewport; protect faces, text and key product details from cropping. Provide meaningful alt text where appropriate, and empty alt text for decorative imagery. Prefer optimized responsive formats, srcset, width and height metadata. Verify transparent PNG/WebP edges against actual backgrounds. Do not invent product photos or prices as if they came from a supplier.
